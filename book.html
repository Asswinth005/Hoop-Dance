Quick Answers

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes! Cancel up to 4 hours before class for full credit. Late cancellations may incur a small fee.

What if I'm running late?

Please arrive 5-10 minutes early. Late arrivals may not be admitted after 10 minutes.

Do you offer private sessions?

Yes! Contact us for 1-on-1 coaching or small group privates starting at $75/hour.
