Quick Answers

Everything you need to know before visiting us

Do I need to book in advance?

Yes, we recommend booking online to secure your spot. Drop-ins are welcome if space permits, but pre-booking is best!

What's your cancellation policy?

Cancel up to 4 hours before class for full credit. Late cancellations may be charged a small fee.

Is the studio accessible?

Absolutely! Our studio is wheelchair accessible with ramps and wide doorways. Contact us for specific accommodations.

Can I book a private hoop party?

Yes! We host birthdays, bachelorettes, and corporate events. Email events@hoopandflow.com for custom packages.

Still have questions?

Come visit us in person or claim your free intro hoop class! We'd love to meet you.
