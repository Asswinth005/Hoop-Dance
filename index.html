Our Hoop Story

Where Hula Hoop Dance Meets Magic

Founded in 2018 by professional flow artist Lena Hoops, Hoop&Flow is a sanctuary for self-expression through hula hoop dance. We believe that hoop flow is not just a workout — it's a form of meditation, a creative outlet, and a joyful community experience.
