Our Story

Where Hoop Dreams
Take Flight

More than a studio — we're a movement. A sanctuary for self-expression, creativity, and the pure joy of hula hoop dance.

Meet the Founder

Lena Hernandez

A professional flow artist with over a decade of experience, Lena discovered hula hooping during a challenging chapter of her life. What started as a playful escape quickly blossomed into a transformative daily practice.

In 2018, she opened Hoop&Flow with a beautifully simple belief: everyone can hoop, and everyone deserves to feel the profound freedom of movement.

"Hooping saved my soul. Now I want to help others find theirs."

Lena Hernandez
