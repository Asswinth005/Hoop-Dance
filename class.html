Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need my own hoop?

Nope! We have a variety of hoops for all sizes and levels. You're welcome to bring your own too.

What should I wear?

Comfortable athletic wear — leggings, tank tops, or shorts. Avoid zippers or buttons that might snag.

I'm not coordinated. Can I still join?

Absolutely! Our beginner classes are designed for people who've never picked up a hoop. Everyone starts somewhere!

How do I book a class?

Click "Book now" on any class card or use our mobile app. First class is free for new students!

First Class Is On Us!

New students get their first hoop class completely free. Come spin with us!
